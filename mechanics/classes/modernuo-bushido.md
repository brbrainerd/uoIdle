# ModernUO Bushido Reference Mechanics: Weapon Parry, Perfection, Stances, and Strikes

Source: [raw](../_raw/modernuo/BaseWeapon.cs)

## Summary

The canonical ModernUO Bushido combat system integrates eight interlocking Samurai mechanics directly into the core weapon swing, hit-chance, damage-amplification, and damage-absorption pipelines. Unlike traditional shield-bearing warriors, a Samurai wielding a one-handed or two-handed melee weapon without a shield derives weapon parry probability from the product of Parry and Bushido skill, whereas equipping a physical shield actively penalizes parry probability as Bushido rises. Surrounding this shieldless weapon parry core are the Honor and Perfection consecutive-hit damage ramp ([[bushido-perfection-honor]]), three mutually exclusive defensive stances—Confidence ([[bushido-confidence]]), Counter Attack ([[bushido-counter-attack]]), and Evasion ([[bushido-evasion]])—and three weapon special moves—Honorable Execution ([[bushido-honorable-execution]]), Lightning Strike ([[bushido-lightning-strike]]), and Momentum Strike ([[bushido-momentum-strike]]). Additionally, the weapon combat loop hosts shared Ninjitsu hooks for Mirror Image clone diversion, Surprise Attack defense shred, and Animal Form combat bonuses ([[modernuo-ninjitsu]]).

## Weapon Parry Scaling Benchmarks (`BaseWeapon.CheckParry`, **C2–C4**)

Assuming `Dex >= 80` (**C4**):
- **Pure Bushido (`100.0` Bushido, `0.0` Parry, No Shield):** `aosChance = 0/800 = 0%`, `chance = 0 + 0.05 = 5.0%` (**C3**).
- **1H Weapon (`Katana` / `Wakizashi` / `Bokuto`) at `100.0` Bushido + `100.0` Parry (No Shield):** `chance = (100 * 100) / 48000.0 + 0.05 = 25.83%` (`34.10%` during `Evasion` at `1.32x`, **C3**, **C4**, `[[bushido-evasion]] C5`).
- **2H Weapon (`NoDachi` / `Daisho` / `Tetsubo`) at `100.0` Bushido + `100.0` Parry (No Shield):** `chance = (100 * 100) / 41140.0 + 0.05 = 29.31%` (`38.69%` during `Evasion` at `1.32x`, **C3**, **C4**, `[[bushido-evasion]] C5`).
- **2H Weapon at `120.0` Bushido + `120.0` Parry (No Shield):** `chance = (120 * 120) / 41140.0 + 0.05 = 40.00%` (`60.00%` during `Evasion` at `1.50x` with GM Anatomy & Tactics, **C3**, **C4**, `[[bushido-evasion]] C5`).
- **Shield Equipped at `100.0` Bushido + `100.0` Parry (`shield != null`):** `chance = Math.Max((100 - 100) / 400.0, 0) + 0.05 = 5.0%` (**C2** — canonical anti-synergy between shields and Bushido).
