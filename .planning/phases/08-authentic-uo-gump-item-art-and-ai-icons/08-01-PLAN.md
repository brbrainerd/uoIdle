# Phase 8 Plan 01: Authentic UO Gump/Item Sprite Extraction & High-Detail AI Raster Iconography

## Objective
Eliminate all procedural `ImageDraw.polygon` icon generation and replace every Samurai and Ninja ability icon, paperdoll character render, and equipment/weapon/tool slot icon with authentic in-game UO gumps (`ability-icons.png`, `art.png`, `paperdoll/gumps/*.png`) and high-detail oil-painted dark-fantasy Tokuno raster artwork, audited via Computer Vision (`read_file`).

## Tasks
1. **Extract Authentic UO Gumps, Item Sprites & Paperdoll Layer Gumps (`ART-01`, `ART-03`)**:
   - Slice authentic ability gumps (`gump_21536`, `gump_21539`, `gump_21540`, `gump_21280`, `gump_21281`, `gump_21286`, `gump_21287`, `gump_20992..21015`, `idle_samurai_way`, `idle_assassin_shadow`, `idle_assassin_eviscerate`) from `ability-icons.png`.

   - Composite authentic layered Ultima Online paperdoll character renders (`paperdoll-samurai.png` and `paperdoll-ninja.png`) from `paperdoll.json` male gump layers (`12.png` base body + equipped Tokuno armor, cloak, and weapons).
2. **Generate High-Detail Oil-Painted Tokuno Ability Sheets (`ART-02`)**:
   - Generate `bushido-sheet.jpg` (2x3 oil-painted Samurai icons) and `ninjitsu-sheet.jpg` (3x3 oil-painted Ninja icons) via `image-generator` and store under `mockups/assets/icons/sources/`.

3. **Computer Vision Visual Audit**:
