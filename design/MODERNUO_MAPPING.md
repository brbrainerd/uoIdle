## Section 1 — Architectural Paradigm Translation (2D Active Spatial UO $\rightarrow$ 1D Automated Tick-Loop UO Idle)

Canonical Ultima Online Samurai Empire (`Expansion.SE`) and Mondain's Legacy (`Core.ML`) Bushido and Ninjitsu mechanics in `modernuo/ModernUO` (`b0e6b17c5cba3a6944ca25a9676dcdec0022ac38`) are built around an interactive 2D spatial world where a live player manually targets monsters, steps across map tiles, manages out-of-combat stealth step budgets, and reloads physical projectiles in a backpack. In contrast, UO Idle (`v0.99.0`) executes combat inside an automated, stationary 1D tick loop (`[[tick-and-cooldowns]]`, `[[engine-capability-matrix]]`).

### 1.2 Why Unadapted Direct Ports Fail in 24/7 AFK Idle Combat

1. **Manual Pre-Combat Honor Targeting (`HonorContext.cs`):** In an automated dungeon hunt where monsters spawn every `20s–50s` (`[[tick-and-cooldowns]] C2`), the player cannot click a target cursor before the first swing. Without automatic target binding, `PerfectionDamageBonus` (`[[bushido-perfection-honor]] C2`) would permanently remain at `0%`.
2. **Honorable Execution's `-40` All-Resistance Failure Penalty (`HonorableExecution.cs:59-73`):** In canonical ModernUO, if `HonorableExecution` fails to kill the target on that exact swing, the Samurai suffers `-40` Physical/Fire/Cold/Poison/Energy resistance and `-100%` `MagicResist` for `7.0s` (`[[bushido-honorable-execution]] C3`). In an automated tick loop against high-HP bosses—or when the hotbar fires slightly above lethal threshold—a `-40` all-resistance self-debuff causes instant one-shot deaths (`REQUIREMENTS.md` Out of Scope). Instead, UO Idle binds `Honorable Execution` to the `enemy hp <= 30%` hotbar trigger predicate (`executeThresholdHpPct: 30`) and rewards kills with the canonical `20.0s` swing-speed and heal buff (`[[bushido-honorable-execution]] C2`) **without** applying the suicidal `-40` resistance penalty on non-lethal hits.
3. **Unthrottled `Mirror Image` Clone Spam (`MirrorImage.cs`):** In canonical ModernUO, `MirrorImage` has a `1.5s` cast delay, costs `10` Mana, and diverts `Ninjitsu / 150.0` (`66.7%–80.0%`) of incoming melee hits onto clones (`[[ninjitsu-mirror-image]] C1`, `[[modernuo-bushido]] C9`). Against a boss swinging every `2.5s`, an unthrottled idle bot recasting `MirrorImage` every `2.0s` would achieve near-`100%` damage immunity. Enforcing an explicit cooldown (`12,000ms–15,000ms`) and capped `clone_stacks` (`1..3` ablative charges with per-clone interception ICD) preserves `Mirror Image` as Ninja's signature mitigation layer without breaking boss EHP balance.
4. **Stationary `Death Strike` & `Ki Attack` Tile Checks (`DeathStrike.cs`, `KiAttack.cs`):** Because UO Idle combat has no 2D tile coordinates, a literal check of `Steps >= 5` (`[[ninjitsu-death-strike]] C3`) or `Math.Sqrt(xDelta^2 + yDelta^2)` (`[[ninjitsu-ki-attack]] C4`) would always evaluate to `0` tiles moved, locking `Death Strike` to its penalized stationary cap (`30` damage) and `Ki Attack` to `1.0x` damage (`0` bonus). Translating `Death Strike` to a **5-swing combat hit counter** (`5` weapon swings within `5.0s`) and `Ki Attack` to a **cooldown-gated gap-closer strike** preserves the exact burst cadence and skill scaling in 1D combat.

### 3.2 Detailed Per-Mechanic Bushido Specifications & Mode B C# Hooks

#### 1. Weapon Parry (`BaseWeapon.CheckParry` — `[[modernuo-bushido]] C2`..`C4`)
- **Canonical Formula:**
  $$\text{ParryChance}_{\text{no shield}} = \max\!\left(\frac{\text{Parry} \times \text{Bushido}}{\text{OneHanded} ? 48000 : 41140} + \mathbb{I}(\text{Parry} \ge 100 \lor \text{Bushido} \ge 100) \times 0.05,\; \frac{\text{Parry}}{800} + \mathbb{I}(\text{Parry} \ge 100) \times 0.05\right)$$
  Multiplied by `Evasion.GetParryScalar(defender)` if evading, and by $(20 + \text{DEX}) / 100$ if $\text{DEX} < 80$.
- **Mode A Specification (`samurai_way` at `L1`):**
  - `id: "samurai_way"`, `kind: "Passive"`, `unlockLevel: 1`, `progressionTreeLevel: 1`, `manaCost: 0`, `cooldownMs: 0`, `effectType: "self_buff"`.
  - Grants passive weapon-parry mitigation (`evasion_pct: 25`, `defense_pct: 15`) when wielding `Katana`, `Wakizashi`, `NoDachi`, or `Daisho` without a shield.

  - `statusEffect`: `durationMs: 10000`, `stackingRule: "replace"`, `modifiers: [{ stat: "perfection_streak_pct", value: 40 }, { stat: "damage_dealt_pct", value: 25 }, { stat: "crit_chance_pct", value: 10 }]`.
